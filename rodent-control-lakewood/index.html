A city built around its lakes.

Lakewood takes its name from the several lakes within its city limits, including American Lake and Gravelly Lake. The city includes a wide mix of housing types, from older lakefront homes to newer apartment developments and a substantial commercial corridor along Bridgeport Way and South Tacoma Way. Our coverage spans all three Lakewood ZIP codes, 98499, 98439 and 98498, so tell us your ZIP when you call and we will confirm coverage and timing.

The city also sits close to Joint Base Lewis-McChord, and some Lakewood addresses near the base perimeter see rodent pressure tied to the same wooded buffer land that affects other communities near the installation.

What we handle on calls from Lakewood.

Calls from Lakewood describe familiar seasonal patterns: mice moving indoors as fall rains set in, rats near a lakeshore or wooded lot line, and dead-rodent odors in a wall after an unnoticed problem runs its course. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for indoor activity, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity common near Lakewood's many lakes and green spaces.

Sealing homes near lakes and greenbelts.

Homes near American Lake, Gravelly Lake or one of Lakewood's smaller lakes tend to have more natural cover close to the foundation than a typical in-town lot, and that cover gives rodents easier access to crawl space vents and garage thresholds. Our rodent exclusion service closes those gaps with hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam, which holds up poorly in a damp lakeside setting.

When an earlier problem has already chewed through insulation or ductwork, our rodent damage repair service repairs that damage once the animals are cleared and entry points are closed.

Yards, docks and outbuildings near the water.

Properties backing onto one of Lakewood's lakes often have a dock, shed or boathouse in addition to the main home, and each of these structures is a potential entry point worth checking on its own. Our backyard and outdoor rodent control service inspects and treats outdoor structures across the full property, not just the area immediately next to the house.

A car, boat or trailer parked near dense shoreline vegetation for more than a few days is an easy target for a nest, and Lakewood's lake-adjacent driveways see this pattern often. Our vehicle and car rodent removal service checks the engine bay and cabin air intake for nesting material and chewed wiring, whether it is a daily driver or a boat that sits between weekend trips.

Active chewing near wiring, a nest close to a furnace, or a burrow undermining a dock or shed footing are reasons to say so clearly when you call. The line is open 24/7, and we prioritize those situations against the regular schedule rather than making you wait for the next open slot.
