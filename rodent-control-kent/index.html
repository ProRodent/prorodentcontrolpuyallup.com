A large city with a valley floor and hillside neighborhoods.

Kent is one of the larger cities in our coverage area, spanning a wide industrial and warehouse district on the valley floor as well as residential neighborhoods on the surrounding hillsides. Our coverage spans all four Kent ZIP codes, 98032, 98030, 98031 and 98042, so tell us your ZIP when you call and we will confirm coverage and timing for your part of the city.

The valley floor includes the Green River and a significant concentration of warehouses and distribution centers, while the hillside neighborhoods are more residential, with a mix of older and newer housing stock.

What we handle on calls from Kent.

Calls from Kent cover a wide range given the city's size, from mice moving into a hillside home as fall rains set in, to rats near a valley-floor drainage ditch, to warehouse and loading-dock activity tied to stored goods. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for indoor residential activity, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity common near the Green River corridor and valley drainage.

Sealing hillside and valley-floor homes.

Kent's hillside neighborhoods include both older housing stock with settled sills and foundations, and newer development where utility penetrations sometimes go unsealed during construction. Our rodent exclusion service closes these gaps with hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam, which holds up poorly through a wet Pacific Northwest winter.

When an earlier problem has already chewed through insulation or wiring, our rodent damage repair service repairs that damage once the animals are cleared and entry points are closed.

Warehouses and distribution centers on the valley floor.

Kent's valley floor is home to a significant number of warehouses and distribution centers, and rodent activity in these facilities poses real risk to stored inventory, and can trigger compliance issues under health and safety inspections during a routine or surprise audit. Our commercial rodent control service covers inspection, treatment and ongoing monitoring for these facilities, including documentation suitable for a health inspector, insurer or corporate compliance requirement.

Vehicles near loading docks and green space.

Vehicles and equipment parked near loading docks, storage yards or greenbelt-adjacent driveways for extended periods are common targets for a nest in Kent's mixed valley-floor and hillside geography. Our vehicle and car rodent removal service checks the engine bay and cabin air intake for nesting material and chewed wiring.

Apartment and multi-unit properties.

Kent includes a substantial share of apartment and multi-unit housing, and we regularly work with property managers coordinating access across several units on one visit. Shared walls and plumbing chases often mean activity in one unit traces back to a building-wide entry point rather than something specific to that single unit, so a coordinated inspection tends to solve the problem faster than treating units one at a time.

When to call it an emergency.

Active chewing near wiring, a nest close to a furnace, or a rodent in an occupied living space or active warehouse are reasons to flag a call as urgent. The line is open 24/7, and we prioritize those situations against the regular schedule rather than making you wait for the next open slot.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any suspected nest.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Do not set out loose bait or poison before we arrive.
  • Note where and when you saw or heard activity.
  • Clear access to the crawl space, garage or facility area if you can do so safely.
