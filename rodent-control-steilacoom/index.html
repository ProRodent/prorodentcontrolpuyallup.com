Washington's first incorporated town.

According to the town's own official history, Steilacoom was Washington's first incorporated town, established in 1854. That history gives the town a distinct, older housing stock compared to newer suburban development elsewhere in our coverage area, with many homes built well before current construction standards and sealing practices existed.

Our coverage includes the full 98388 ZIP, so whether your home sits near the historic downtown or in a newer section of town, tell us your address when you call and we will confirm coverage and timing.

What we handle on calls from Steilacoom.

Calls from Steilacoom often trace back to the town's older housing stock: settled gaps at foundations, chimneys and additions that have opened up gradually over many decades. Combined with the shoreline setting, that creates a mix of indoor mouse activity and outdoor rat activity near the water and wooded lots. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for indoor activity, and rat control addresses the outdoor, ground-level pattern common near the shoreline and nearby wooded parcels.

Sealing Steilacoom's older housing stock.

Homes in a town with this much history often have foundations, additions and chimney surrounds that have shifted gradually over the decades, opening gaps that were never a problem when the home was newer. Our rodent exclusion service closes these gaps with hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam, materials built to hold up through repeated wet seasons on an older structure.

When an earlier problem has already chewed through insulation or wiring in one of these older homes, our rodent damage repair service repairs that damage once the animals are cleared and entry points are closed.
