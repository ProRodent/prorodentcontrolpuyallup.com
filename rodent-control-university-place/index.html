A bluff-top lot doesn't seal the way a flat one does.

The terrain does a lot of the work here. A house perched near a ravine or the bluff overlooking the Sound simply has more brush and slope right up against its foundation than a flat in-town lot would, and every bit of that extra cover is a potential path to a crawl space vent. What we've found works best is rodent exclusion that leans on hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam, which tends to break down fastest in exactly this kind of damp, wooded setting.

For damage that's already been done, chewed insulation or wiring from an earlier problem, rodent damage repair picks up the repair work once the animals are gone and the opening is closed.
