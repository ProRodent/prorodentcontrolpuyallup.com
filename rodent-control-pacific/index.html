Pacific sits on the county line.

Pacific is its own incorporated city, with parts of the community falling in Pierce County and parts in King County depending on the exact address. That county-line split does not change how we handle a call, since our coverage is based on the 98047 ZIP rather than which county side an address happens to sit on. Tell us your address when you call and we will confirm coverage either way.

The city sits along the White River, and low-lying ground near the river shares some of the same conditions we see near the Puyallup River, including damp soil that draws rats along drainage corridors toward nearby buildings.

What we handle on calls from Pacific.

Calls from Pacific addresses often describe rat activity near a ditch or fence line, mice moving into a garage as the weather turns, or a smell that points to a dead rodent in a wall or crawl space. Our rat control service addresses the outdoor, ground-level pattern common near the river, while mouse control covers the indoor side of the same seasonal shift.

Sealing older Pacific homes.

Pacific's older housing stock, much of it built well before current construction standards, tends to have settled gaps at sills, chimneys and additions. Our rodent exclusion service closes those gaps with hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam, which holds up poorly through a wet Pacific Northwest winter.

If chewed insulation or ductwork has already resulted from an earlier rodent problem, our rodent damage repair service covers putting that material right once the active animals are cleared and the entry point is sealed.

Yards backing onto the White River corridor.

Properties with a yard backing onto the White River or a connected drainage feature see more ground-level rat activity than homes farther from water. Sheds and outbuildings near the river edge are often the first sign of activity, since a gap under a shed floor sitting close to prime rat habitat gets found before the main house does. Checking that structure first, before assuming the problem starts at the house, often saves a callback later.

Small businesses along Pacific's main streets.

Pacific's small commercial strip includes shops and food-related businesses that face similar seasonal pressure to nearby homes. Our commercial rodent control service covers inspection and monitoring for these properties, including documentation if you need it for a landlord or health inspector.

When to call it an emergency.

Active chewing near wiring, a nest close to a furnace, or a burrow that has undermined a shed or deck footing are reasons to say so clearly when you call. The line is open 24/7, and we prioritize those situations against the regular schedule.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any suspected nest or burrow.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Do not set out loose bait or poison before we arrive.
