Camp Murray is a military headquarters, not a town.

Camp Murray is the headquarters of the Washington National Guard and the Washington Military Department, sitting adjacent to Joint Base Lewis-McChord. It is not an incorporated town, has no civilian mayor or city council, and we are not going to write it up as an ordinary residential community.

The 98430 ZIP appears in our coverage data, and we take calls associated with it, but Camp Murray itself is a controlled military facility. Access for outside service providers depends on the facility's own security procedures rather than a standard residential visit, and we tell callers that honestly rather than implying we can walk onto the property the way we would a regular home.

What we can realistically offer.

If you have a rodent concern connected to a Camp Murray facility, the first call should generally go to the installation's own facilities or logistics office. Work on a state military headquarters typically needs to be coordinated and authorized through official channels before any outside contractor is brought in. We are glad to discuss the situation by phone and explain what a visit would involve if authorization is arranged, but we do not want to overstate what we can do without that authorization in place.
