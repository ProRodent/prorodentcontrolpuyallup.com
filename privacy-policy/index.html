Who this applies to.

This policy covers the website for Puyallup Pro Rodent Control. It is a general policy written in plain language. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace the privacy terms of any third party mentioned here.

What this site does not collect.

This site has no forms, no accounts, no comment sections and no email sign-up. We do not ask you to type your name, address or phone number into the site, and the site itself does not collect contact details from you.

Phone calls and email.

Every request for service starts with a phone call. When you call, we will ask for information needed to help you, such as your street, the location of the problem and how to reach you. We use that only to arrange and carry out service. If you email us instead, we use the information in that message the same way.

Calls to the number on this site may be routed through a call-tracking or telephony provider. That provider may log call details such as the time, the caller's number and the duration. Calls may be recorded, and where the law requires it we will tell you at the start of the call. If you do not want to be recorded, say so and we will address it.

Embedded map.

The service area page shows an embedded Google Maps frame. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which may set cookies or receive technical information such as your IP address. We do not control what Google collects. Google's own privacy policy explains how it handles that data.

Analytics, hosting and cookies.

Websites are normally served through a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, for security and performance. Our pages may also use cookies or similar tools from the call-tracking provider or the map. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

How information is used and shared.

We use information from calls and emails to schedule visits, perform the work and follow up. We do not sell personal information that we collect ourselves. We may share what is needed with providers who help us run the service, such as telephony or scheduling tools, and where the law requires it.

We cannot make promises about how third-party providers handle data, so please read their policies if you have concerns. We keep call and email information only as long as needed for service, record keeping and legal obligations.

Children.

This site is meant for adults arranging pest control. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact.

We may update this policy, and the effective date above will change when we do. For privacy questions, call the number shown on this site or use the email address in the footer.
